Holographic avatar (package com.jarvis.android.avatar)

This avatar is an Android adaptation of the avatar of MARK LIV by FatihMakes
(https://github.com/FatihMakes/Mark-LIV), licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International licence (CC BY-NC 4.0).
Commercial use of this avatar, and of any app that includes it, is not permitted.

Taken from Mark-LIV: the head mesh builder (cranium, neck, jaw/brow/lip weights, landmark rings; run once,
its result is stored in head_mesh.bin), the animation model (jaw, lips, brows, gaze, blinks, state expression),
the text-to-viseme table and the audio formant analysis that drive the lip-sync.
Changed or added here: Kotlin port, drawing on Android's canvas, a lip-sync clock tied to what the speaker plays,
rim light, scan lines, lids that follow the assistant's state.

The head geometry inside head_mesh.bin comes from "Infinite, 3D Head Scan" by Lee Perry-Smith (Infinite Realities,
https://triplegangers.com), Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), as shipped with the
three.js examples (https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js). It was resampled, normalised, cut at the neck and rigged by
tools/avatar/export_head.py; the eyes and lips are placed with the landmark positions of MediaPipe's canonical face model
(https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe), Apache License 2.0, Copyright 2019-2023 The MediaPipe Authors.

The original head is unchanged in head_mesh.bin (tools/avatar/export_head.py).

The face "Léa" (head_mesh_lea.bin) is based on "Female Head Sculpt"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/female-head-sculpt-ae24c33594a046519014fdc78758a8ec) by Aconear (https://sketchfab.com/Aconear),
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0, http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: turned to face
forward, welded and simplified from 1.36 million to about 22,000 triangles (tools/avatar/prepare_sculpt.py), normalised, cut at the neck,
given eye openings with eyeballs, a mouth with teeth, the rig and the landmark rings of Mark-LIV's builder, and hair
(tools/avatar/export_head.py, tools/avatar/groom.py); the renderer gives her a silver "android" skin, etched circuits and a halo.

The face "Marc" (head_mesh_marc.bin) is based on "Realistic Male head"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/realistic-male-head-8935543c732c454c932af6101ae5a190) by Ouail (https://sketchfab.com/OuailArt),
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0, http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: welded and simplified
from 2.26 million to about 20,000 triangles (tools/avatar/prepare_sculpt.py), normalised, cut at the neck on a tilted plane, given eye
openings with eyeballs, a mouth with teeth, the rig and the landmark rings of Mark-LIV's builder, and short hair (tools/avatar/export_head.py,
tools/avatar/groom.py).
