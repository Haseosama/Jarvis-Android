Sky and map data used by Jarvis (assets/sky)

stars.tsv
  The Yale Bright Star Catalog, 5th revised edition (Hoffleit & Warren, 1991), as distributed by the Harvard-Smithsonian Center for
  Astrophysics (http://tdc-www.harvard.edu/catalogs/bsc5.html): stars down to visual magnitude 5.0, J2000 positions.

land.bin, borders.bin, cities.tsv
  Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com), public domain: 1:50m land, country boundary lines and populated places, simplified
  (Douglas-Peucker, 0.02°) and stored as 1/100 degree integers; cities of 150 000 people or more, and the capitals.

Computed on the phone, not bundled:
  planets from JPL's "Approximate Positions of the Planets" (E. M. Standish), the Moon from the Astronomical Almanac's low-precision
  formulae, satellites with SGP4 (Vallado et al., 2006) from CelesTrak's public orbits.
